Samos Core privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

What Samos Core accesses

With your consent, Samos Core requests read-only access to your Gmail (gmail.readonly) and Google Calendar events (calendar.events.readonly). It cannot send, change, or delete mail or events.

How the data is used

Samos Core runs on your own computer. When you, or an assistant you have authorized, asks for mail or calendar information, the gateway on your computer retrieves it from Google and returns it to that requester. The data is used only to show you your own information.

Storage and sharing

  • Your Google credential is stored in your own password manager on your computer. It is not sent to the operator of Samos Core.
  • Samos Core does not store mail or calendar content. An AI assistant you connect, such as Claude, receives the content it requests and processes it under its provider's own terms.
  • Samos Core does not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than the assistant you connect.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Samos Core's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke Samos Core's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
